THE MISSION

Real engineering. Measurable evidence.

Make useful processor architecture discoverable, testable, and eventually manufacturable through autonomous engineering.

Can an AI research system propose a hardware architecture, verify its correctness, and demonstrate measurable gains over an honest baseline?

We treat AI-generated engineering ideas as hypotheses, not accomplishments. The research loop demands explicit assumptions, independent tests, reproducible artifacts, and comparison with strong conventional methods. No performance claim is published without a traceable experiment.

THE RESEARCH AGENDA

Three frontiers.
One rigorous method.

A focused technical agenda, structured to produce useful open research artifacts rather than speculative demonstrations.

01 / ARCHITECTURE

Domain-specific compute

Explore small RISC-V extensions, low-power accelerators, and fixed-latency datapaths for real-time systems.

02 / AUTOMATION

Agent-directed EDA

Connect structured agent proposals to RTL generation, testbenches, synthesis, and design-space exploration.

03 / EVIDENCE

Measured performance

Compare area, timing, estimated power, and functional correctness with versioned artifacts.

How each research cycle works

01

DESIGN

02

03

Run synthesis and physical-design estimates with reproducible toolchains.

04

EVOLVE

Retain evidence-backed improvements; reject candidates that fail verification.
